## 📥 Cara Download & Gunakan Foto

### Option 1: Free Stock Photo Sites
1. **Unsplash** (https://unsplash.com)
   - 100% gratis, no attribution required
   - High quality photos
   - Commercial use allowed

2. **Pexels** (https://www.pexels.com)
   - Free untuk commercial use
   - Large collection
   - No attribution needed

3. **Pixabay** (https://pixabay.com)
   - Free images & videos
   - No attribution required
   - Diverse content

### Option 2: Creative Commons (Dengan Attribution)
1. **Flickr Creative Commons** (https://www.flickr.com/creativecommons/)
2. **Wikimedia Commons** (https://commons.wikimedia.org/)
3. **Google Images** dengan filter "Creative Commons licenses"

### Option 3: Fotografi Sendiri
- Paling authentic untuk Godong Ijo
- Sesuai dengan actual programs
- Lokasi & setting asli
- Perlu koordinasi photo session

## 🎨 Editing & Optimization

### Tools Rekomendasi:
1. **Squoosh.app** - Convert & compress to WebP
2. **TinyPNG.com** - Compress JPEG/PNG
3. **Canva.com** - Edit & resize
4. **Photopea.com** - Free Photoshop alternative

### Specifications:
- Format: .webp (primary) + .jpg (fallback)
- Size: Max 200KB per file
- Dimensions: 800x800px or 1200x800px
- Quality: 80-85% for webp, 85-90% for jpg
