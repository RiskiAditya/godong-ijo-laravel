## 📐 Format Spesifikasi

### File Format
- **Primary:** `.webp` (optimal compression)
- **Fallback:** `.jpg` (browser compatibility)

### Size & Dimension
- **Square cards:** 800x800px (1:1)
- **Landscape cards:** 1200x800px (3:2)
- **Max file size:** 200KB (compressed)

### Quality
- **WebP:** 80-85% quality
- **JPEG:** 85-90% quality

## 🎨 Style Guide (Singkat)

### ✅ DO
- Natural lighting
- Kids looking happy & engaged
- Action shots (hands-on activity)
- Clean background
- Authentic moments

### ❌ DON'T
- Overly posed
- Dark/underexposed
- Cluttered background
- Stock photo vibe

---

## 🔄 Steps Update Foto

1. **Ambil/edit foto** sesuai spesifikasi
2. **Convert to WebP** (squoosh.app)
3. **Compress** (<200KB)
4. **Upload** ke `public/images/education/`
5. **Clear cache:** `php artisan cache:clear`
6. **Test** di browser
