# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Email Notification System provides automated email communications for customers and administrators throughout the booking lifecycle in The Waterfall tourism booking platform. The system sends transactional emails at key lifecycle events (booking submission, payment confirmation, cancellation) to enhance customer experience and operational awareness. The system must integrate seamlessly with Laravel's existing BookingController, support multiple email service providers (Gmail SMTP, SendGrid, Mailtrap), handle failures gracefully, and provide both synchronous and asynchronous (queued) email delivery.

## Glossary

- **Email_Service**: The Laravel mail service that sends emails via configured providers (SMTP, SendGrid, Mailtrap, Mailpit)
- **Notification_System**: The complete email notification system including templates, services, and event listeners
- **Customer_Email**: Transactional email sent to the customer's email address from the booking form
- **Admin_Email**: Notification email sent to the administrator's configured email address
- **Email_Template**: HTML-based Blade template file for rendering email content with dynamic data
- **Email_Queue**: Laravel queue system for asynchronous email delivery (database or Redis-backed)
- **Booking_Event**: Laravel event fired at specific points in the booking lifecycle (created, paid, cancelled)
- **E_Ticket**: PDF file attachment generated by ETicketService containing QR code and booking details
- **Email_Provider**: External email service (Gmail SMTP, SendGrid, Mailtrap) configured via environment variables
- **Mailpit**: Local email testing tool that captures emails during development (port 1025)
- **Mailtrap**: Staging email testing service for pre-production email verification
- **Email_Failure**: Condition where email delivery fails due to network, provider, or configuration issues
- **Booking_Confirmation_Email**: Email sent immediately after customer submits booking form (status: pending)
- **Payment_Success_Email**: Email sent after payment is confirmed by Midtrans (status: paid, includes e-ticket)
- **Payment_Instructions_Email**: Email sent for bank transfer payment methods containing transfer instructions
- **Cancellation_Email**: Email sent when booking status changes to cancelled or expired
- **New_Booking_Alert**: Email sent to admin when a customer submits a new booking
- **Payment_Received_Notification**: Email sent to admin when payment is successfully confirmed

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Customer Booking Confirmation Email

**User Story:** As a customer, I want to receive a confirmation email immediately after submitting my booking, so that I have proof of my booking submission and payment instructions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a booking is successfully created (BookingController::store returns success), THE Notification_System SHALL send a Booking_Confirmation_Email to the Customer_Email address
2. THE Booking_Confirmation_Email SHALL include the kode_booking, nama_lengkap, paket wisata name, tanggal_kunjungan, jumlah_orang, and total_harga
3. THE Booking_Confirmation_Email SHALL include payment instructions with the order_id and payment deadline (24 hours from booking creation)
4. THE Booking_Confirmation_Email SHALL use a responsive HTML Email_Template with The Waterfall branding (logo, colors, footer)
5. THE Booking_Confirmation_Email SHALL be written in Indonesian language
6. THE Booking_Confirmation_Email SHALL include a link to check booking status using the kode_booking
7. WHEN the email address is invalid or malformed, THE Email_Service SHALL reject the email and log the validation error
8. THE Notification_System SHALL deliver the Booking_Confirmation_Email within 30 seconds of booking creation for sync delivery
9. WHERE Email_Queue is enabled, THE Notification_System SHALL queue the Booking_Confirmation_Email for async delivery
10. IF Email_Service delivery fails, THEN THE Notification_System SHALL log the failure with kode_booking and error details without blocking the booking transaction

### Requirement 2: Customer Payment Success Email with E-Ticket

**User Story:** As a customer, I want to receive a payment confirmation email with my e-ticket attached when my payment is successful, so that I have immediate access to my ticket and booking details.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a booking status changes to 'paid' (after Midtrans payment confirmation), THE Notification_System SHALL send a Payment_Success_Email to the Customer_Email address
2. THE Payment_Success_Email SHALL include the kode_booking, transaction_id, payment_type, paid_at timestamp, and booking details
3. THE Payment_Success_Email SHALL attach the E_Ticket PDF file generated by ETicketService
4. THE E_Ticket attachment SHALL be named "E-Ticket-{kode_booking}.pdf"
5. THE Payment_Success_Email SHALL include a download link for the E_Ticket as an alternative to the attachment
6. THE Payment_Success_Email SHALL include QR code image embedded inline for quick venue entry
7. THE Payment_Success_Email SHALL use a responsive HTML Email_Template with success styling (green accents, checkmark icon)
8. THE Payment_Success_Email SHALL be written in Indonesian language
9. IF E_Ticket generation fails, THEN THE Notification_System SHALL send the Payment_Success_Email without the attachment and log the error
10. IF Email_Service delivery fails after 3 retry attempts, THEN THE Notification_System SHALL mark the email as failed and alert the administrator

### Requirement 3: Customer Bank Transfer Payment Instructions Email

**User Story:** As a customer who selected bank transfer payment, I want to receive detailed payment instructions via email, so that I know exactly how to complete my payment.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a booking is created AND payment_type is bank transfer (BCA VA, BNI VA, BRI VA, Permata VA), THE Notification_System SHALL send a Payment_Instructions_Email to the Customer_Email address
2. THE Payment_Instructions_Email SHALL include the virtual account number provided by Midtrans
3. THE Payment_Instructions_Email SHALL include the exact payment amount (gross_amount) and payment deadline
4. THE Payment_Instructions_Email SHALL include step-by-step transfer instructions specific to the selected bank
5. THE Payment_Instructions_Email SHALL include a warning that payment must be completed before the deadline to avoid cancellation
6. THE Payment_Instructions_Email SHALL use a responsive HTML Email_Template with clear formatting and bank logos
7. THE Payment_Instructions_Email SHALL be written in Indonesian language
8. WHEN payment_type is e-wallet (GoPay, ShopeePay) or QRIS, THE Notification_System SHALL include the appropriate payment instructions in the Booking_Confirmation_Email instead

### Requirement 4: Customer Booking Cancellation Email

**User Story:** As a customer, I want to receive an email notification if my booking is cancelled or expires, so that I am informed about my booking status and any refund process.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a booking status changes to 'cancelled' or 'expired', THE Notification_System SHALL send a Cancellation_Email to the Customer_Email address
2. THE Cancellation_Email SHALL include the kode_booking, cancellation reason (payment timeout, manual cancellation, system expiry), and cancelled_at timestamp
3. THE Cancellation_Email SHALL include refund information if payment was already received
4. THE Cancellation_Email SHALL include a link to contact customer support for assistance
5. THE Cancellation_Email SHALL use a responsive HTML Email_Template with neutral styling (gray accents)
6. THE Cancellation_Email SHALL be written in Indonesian language
7. WHEN cancellation is triggered by payment timeout (Midtrans expire event), THE Cancellation_Email SHALL include instructions for rebooking

### Requirement 5: Admin New Booking Alert Email

**User Story:** As an administrator, I want to receive an email notification when a new booking is submitted, so that I can monitor bookings in real-time and respond quickly to customer inquiries.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a booking is successfully created, THE Notification_System SHALL send a New_Booking_Alert to the configured Admin_Email address
2. THE New_Booking_Alert SHALL include all booking details (kode_booking, nama_lengkap, email, no_hp, paket wisata, tanggal_kunjungan, jumlah_orang, total_harga)
3. THE New_Booking_Alert SHALL include a direct link to view the booking in the admin panel
4. THE New_Booking_Alert SHALL use a simple HTML Email_Template optimized for quick scanning
5. THE New_Booking_Alert SHALL be written in Indonesian language
6. WHERE Email_Queue is enabled, THE Notification_System SHALL queue the New_Booking_Alert with low priority (after customer emails)
7. THE Admin_Email address SHALL be configurable via environment variable (ADMIN_EMAIL)

### Requirement 6: Admin Payment Received Notification Email

**User Story:** As an administrator, I want to receive an email notification when a payment is successfully confirmed, so that I can track revenue and prepare for customer visits.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a booking status changes to 'paid', THE Notification_System SHALL send a Payment_Received_Notification to the configured Admin_Email address
2. THE Payment_Received_Notification SHALL include the kode_booking, transaction_id, payment_type, payment amount, and paid_at timestamp
3. THE Payment_Received_Notification SHALL include customer contact details (nama_lengkap, email, no_hp)
4. THE Payment_Received_Notification SHALL include a direct link to view the booking in the admin panel
5. THE Payment_Received_Notification SHALL use a simple HTML Email_Template with success indicators
6. THE Payment_Received_Notification SHALL be written in Indonesian language

### Requirement 7: Email Service Provider Configuration

**User Story:** As a system administrator, I want to configure multiple email providers via environment variables, so that I can switch providers without code changes and use different providers for development, staging, and production.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Email_Service SHALL support Gmail SMTP configuration via MAIL_MAILER=smtp with MAIL_HOST, MAIL_PORT, MAIL_USERNAME, MAIL_PASSWORD
2. THE Email_Service SHALL support SendGrid configuration via MAIL_MAILER=sendgrid with SENDGRID_API_KEY
3. THE Email_Service SHALL support Mailtrap configuration for staging testing via MAIL_MAILER=smtp with Mailtrap credentials
4. THE Email_Service SHALL support Mailpit configuration for local development via MAIL_MAILER=smtp with host=localhost and port=1025
5. THE Email_Service SHALL read MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS and MAIL_FROM_NAME from environment variables for sender identity
6. THE Email_Service SHALL validate required configuration variables on application startup and throw configuration exception if missing
7. WHERE multiple email providers are configured, THE Email_Service SHALL use the provider specified by MAIL_MAILER variable
8. THE Email_Service SHALL support different providers for different email types (transactional vs marketing) via configuration

### Requirement 8: Asynchronous Email Queue System

**User Story:** As a system administrator, I want emails to be sent asynchronously via queue system, so that email delivery does not block HTTP responses and booking performance is maintained.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHERE QUEUE_CONNECTION is set to 'database' or 'redis', THE Notification_System SHALL queue all emails for async delivery
2. THE Email_Queue SHALL process queued emails with retry logic (3 attempts with exponential backoff)
3. THE Email_Queue SHALL assign priority levels (high for customer payment success, medium for customer booking confirmation, low for admin alerts)
4. WHERE QUEUE_CONNECTION is 'sync', THE Notification_System SHALL send emails immediately during the HTTP request
5. THE Email_Queue SHALL store failed jobs in failed_jobs table after max retry attempts
6. THE Email_Queue SHALL log all email job attempts with status (pending, processing, completed, failed)
7. WHEN queue worker is not running AND emails are queued, THE Notification_System SHALL log a warning after 5 minutes delay

### Requirement 9: Email Template Rendering and Branding

**User Story:** As a marketing manager, I want all customer emails to have consistent professional branding and responsive design, so that emails display correctly on all devices and reinforce our brand identity.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Email_Template SHALL use Laravel Blade templating engine for dynamic content rendering
2. THE Email_Template SHALL include The Waterfall logo in the header with alt text for accessibility
3. THE Email_Template SHALL use the brand color palette (primary colors from the website CSS)
4. THE Email_Template SHALL be responsive and display correctly on mobile devices (viewport width 320px to 768px)
5. THE Email_Template SHALL include a footer with company contact information (address, phone, email, website)
6. THE Email_Template SHALL include unsubscribe link for marketing emails (not required for transactional emails)
7. THE Email_Template SHALL render correctly in major email clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, Yahoo Mail)
8. THE Email_Template SHALL use inline CSS styles for maximum email client compatibility
9. THE Email_Template SHALL support Indonesian language characters (UTF-8 encoding)
10. WHERE Email_Template rendering fails, THE Email_Service SHALL fall back to plain text email with the same content

### Requirement 10: Email Delivery Error Handling and Logging

**User Story:** As a system administrator, I want comprehensive error handling and logging for email delivery failures, so that I can diagnose issues and ensure critical notifications are not silently lost.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN Email_Service encounters a network error, THE Notification_System SHALL log the error with timestamp, recipient, email type, and full exception stack trace
2. WHEN Email_Service encounters an invalid recipient email address, THE Notification_System SHALL log a validation error without retrying
3. WHEN Email_Service encounters a provider authentication error, THE Notification_System SHALL log a configuration error and alert the administrator
4. WHEN Email_Service rate limit is exceeded, THE Notification_System SHALL queue the email for retry after the rate limit reset time
5. IF an email fails after maximum retry attempts, THEN THE Notification_System SHALL store the failed email details in failed_jobs table for manual review
6. THE Notification_System SHALL expose a monitoring endpoint (/admin/email-status) that returns email delivery metrics (sent count, failed count, queue depth)
7. THE Notification_System SHALL log successful email deliveries with recipient, subject, and delivery timestamp for audit purposes
8. WHERE email failure affects critical customer communications (Payment_Success_Email), THE Notification_System SHALL send an alert to Admin_Email

### Requirement 11: Email Preview and Testing Features

**User Story:** As a developer, I want to preview email templates with sample data and test email delivery, so that I can verify email appearance and functionality before deploying to production.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Notification_System SHALL provide an email preview route (/admin/email-preview/{template}) that renders templates with sample data in the browser
2. THE email preview feature SHALL support all email template types (booking confirmation, payment success, cancellation, admin alerts)
3. THE Notification_System SHALL provide a test email sending route (/admin/email-test) that sends test emails to a specified address
4. WHERE MAIL_MAILER is 'mailpit', THE Notification_System SHALL provide a link to the Mailpit UI (http://localhost:8025) in the admin panel
5. WHERE MAIL_MAILER is 'mailtrap', THE Notification_System SHALL provide a link to the Mailtrap inbox in the admin panel
6. THE test email feature SHALL validate the recipient address before attempting delivery
7. THE email preview feature SHALL only be accessible to authenticated administrators (AdminMiddleware required)

### Requirement 12: Email Template Parser and Formatter

**User Story:** As a system maintainer, I want email templates to correctly parse and format booking data (dates, currency, phone numbers), so that customer emails display accurate and localized information.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Email_Template SHALL format currency amounts using Indonesian Rupiah format (Rp 1.500.000) via Laravel's number_format helper
2. THE Email_Template SHALL format dates using Indonesian locale (dd/MM/yyyy format) via Carbon with locale 'id'
3. THE Email_Template SHALL format phone numbers consistently (e.g., 0812-3456-7890) for display
4. THE Email_Template SHALL parse and render booking status labels in Indonesian (Pending, Lunas, Dibatalkan) using model accessor methods
5. THE Email_Template SHALL escape user-provided content (nama_lengkap, email) to prevent XSS vulnerabilities in email HTML
6. WHERE booking data contains null values, THE Email_Template SHALL display appropriate placeholder text (e.g., "Belum tersedia" for missing transaction_id)
7. THE Email_Template SHALL support RTL text rendering for future internationalization (currently not required for Indonesian)
8. FOR ALL valid booking objects, rendering the template then parsing key fields SHALL produce equivalent formatted values (round-trip property for formatters)

### Requirement 13: Email Attachment Handling for E-Tickets

**User Story:** As a customer, I want my e-ticket PDF to be reliably attached to the payment success email, so that I can access my ticket immediately without additional steps.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN generating Payment_Success_Email, THE Notification_System SHALL call ETicketService::generate(kode_booking) to create the E_Ticket PDF
2. THE Notification_System SHALL attach the E_Ticket PDF file using Laravel's ->attach() method with correct MIME type (application/pdf)
3. THE E_Ticket attachment SHALL not exceed 5MB in file size
4. IF E_Ticket generation fails, THEN THE Notification_System SHALL send the email without attachment and include a download link instead
5. THE Notification_System SHALL delete temporary E_Ticket files from storage after successful email delivery
6. WHERE E_Ticket contains a QR code, THE Notification_System SHALL also embed the QR code inline in the email body for immediate scanning
7. THE Notification_System SHALL set appropriate email headers (Content-Disposition: attachment) for the PDF file

### Requirement 14: Email Event Integration with Booking Lifecycle

**User Story:** As a developer, I want email notifications to be triggered automatically by booking lifecycle events, so that the system maintains separation of concerns and emails are always sent at the correct time.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN BookingController::store completes successfully, THE Notification_System SHALL dispatch a BookingCreated event with the Pemesanan model
2. WHEN BookingController::notification updates booking status to 'paid', THE Notification_System SHALL dispatch a PaymentConfirmed event with the Pemesanan and Pembayaran models
3. WHEN BookingController::notification updates booking status to 'cancelled', THE Notification_System SHALL dispatch a BookingCancelled event with the Pemesanan model and cancellation reason
4. THE Notification_System SHALL register event listeners (BookingCreatedListener, PaymentConfirmedListener, BookingCancelledListener) in EventServiceProvider
5. THE event listeners SHALL be asynchronous (implement ShouldQueue interface) to avoid blocking request processing
6. WHERE an event listener throws an exception, THE Email_Queue SHALL retry the listener according to queue retry policy
7. THE Notification_System SHALL log all event dispatches and listener executions for debugging purposes

### Requirement 15: Multi-Language Email Support Foundation

**User Story:** As a product manager, I want the email system to support Indonesian language natively with a foundation for future multi-language support, so that we can expand to international markets without rebuilding the email system.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Email_Template SHALL use Laravel's @lang() directive for all static text strings
2. THE Notification_System SHALL create language files (resources/lang/id/emails.php) for Indonesian email content
3. THE Email_Template SHALL respect the application locale setting (config('app.locale')) when rendering
4. WHERE a translation key is missing, THE Email_Template SHALL fall back to the English translation or the translation key itself
5. THE Notification_System SHALL support future language expansion by loading translations from resources/lang/{locale}/emails.php
6. THE Email_Service SHALL set the email Content-Language header based on the selected locale
7. FOR ALL email templates, switching locale then rendering SHALL produce translated content without affecting data values (locale independence property)
